Quick guide

        
    
          
  1. 1
    Plug in the base stationUse a USB data cable. The app finds the ESP32-C3 by itself; otherwise open Settings and pick the port.
    2. 
          
  2. 2
    Set your positionSettings → Base station position, so the dashboard can show distance and bearing to the falcon.
    3. 
          
  3. 3
    Switch on the tag outdoorsThe first GPS fix takes 30–60 seconds under open sky. Until then the tag reports "no fix" and the last known position.
    4. 
          
  4. 4
    Watch the status cardGreen TRACKING means a fresh fix. Amber means no GPS fix or low battery. Red means no packet for longer than the lost-signal time.
    5. 
          
  5. 5
    If the bird is lostUse the last known position and bearing, press Navigate to open directions on your phone, and walk towards the last position while watching RSSI.
    6. 
        

        
No hardware yet? Settings → Start demo shows two simulated falcons.

      

    


    
    

      

        
About

        

          
ProjectFalcon Tracker

          
HardwareESP32-C3 Super Mini · XL1278-SMT (SX1278) · ATGM336H

          
LinkLoRa 433 MHz, 30-byte frame with CRC-16

          
LicenseMIT

          
Source codegithub.com/SkillsHUB-Electronics/falcon-tracker

        

        
Check your local 433 MHz radio regulations before use, and keep the tag well under 3–5 % of the bird's body weight.
